# Encyclopedia plates: provenance and licences

The Encyclopedia (`fenel/modules/encyclopedia/`) shows two botanical plates as
small Image data (`illustrations-data.fenel`, 72 pixels wide, reduced from
these files by a script). The original files are kept here unchanged.

| `narthex-asafoetida-van-houtte.jpg` | A *Ferula* species. The plate's caption reads "Narthex asafoetida Falconer", "Tibet", "Plein air". Falconer's genus *Narthex* is now part of *Ferula*, the genus of giant fennel (*Ferula communis*); the plant shown is not giant fennel itself. GBIF's backbone lists "Narthex asafoetida Falc." as a synonym of *Ferula narthex* Boiss.; "Narthex asafoetida (L.) Falc. ex Lindl." is listed under *Ferula assa-foetida* L. | 19th-century hand-coloured lithograph; the imprint names Louis van Houtte's workshop, which suggests *Flore des serres et des jardins de l'Europe*. Supplied to the project on 2026-11-06; the exact volume and page are not recorded. 1037 × 1280. | Public domain (19th-century work), as supplied. |

In the data, the Köhler plate belongs to common fennel. The Van Houtte plate
belongs to the genus *Ferula*, not to giant fennel, because it shows another
member of that genus.
